# 🔍 Digital Criminal Investigation Challenge — 11th Competition WriteUp

**Junior Track Gold Prize 🥇**

## About

"디지털 범인을 찾아라 (Find the Digital Criminal)" is an annual digital forensics competition co-hosted by the **(사)한국포렌식학회** and the **한국저작권보호원** since 2015.

Participants receive digital evidence images based on real criminal investigation scenarios, and are evaluated on their ability to collect, preserve, and analyze evidence following legally sound procedures.

The 11th competition (2025) focused on emerging digital crimes including **AI-powered fraud**, **deepfake exploitation**, and **digital intellectual property infringement**, requiring participants to solve complex, multi-layered forensic challenges.

## Track

- **Junior** — Undergraduate students and below
- **Senior** — Professionals and general participants

## Result

> **Junior Track — Gold Prize (금상)**

A total of 13 winners were selected. Awards included commendations from the Prosecutor General, the President of KAFS, and the Director of KCOPA.
